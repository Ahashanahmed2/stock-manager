💡 Wave Pattern Guide
Impulse Waves (1,3,5):
• Terminal: 5-3-5-3-5
• Sharp price movements
• Wave 3 is often the longest
Corrective Waves (2,4):
• Zigzag: 5-3-5 (ABC)
• Flat: 3-3-5 (ABC)
• Often complex patterns
