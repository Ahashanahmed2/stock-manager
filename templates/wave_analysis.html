📖 Wave Pattern Guide
Impulse Waves (1,3,5):
• Pattern: 5-3-5-3-5
• Wave 1: Starting of trend
• Wave 3: Strongest, often extends
• Wave 5: Final push
Terminal/Diagonal:
• Leading Diagonal: 5-3-5-3-5
- Occurs in Wave 1 or A
• Ending Diagonal: 3-3-3-3-3
- Occurs in Wave 5 or C
• Key: Wedge shape, overlapping
Corrective Waves:
• Zigzag (5-3-5): Sharp
- Wave A: 5 waves
- Wave B: 3 waves (can be any pattern)
- Wave C: 5 waves
• Flat (3-3-5): Sideways
- Wave A: 3 waves
- Wave B: 3 waves (often >100% of A)
- Wave C: 5 waves
Sub-wave B Structure:
• In Zigzag: B is always corrective
- Can be zigzag, flat, or triangle
• In Flat: B often strongest
- Can retrace >100% of A
• Always 3 waves structure
